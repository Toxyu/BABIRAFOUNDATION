Page 1 - About Babira Foundation

1.1 Introduction

Babira Foundation is a community-focused non-profit organization committed to improving the lives and opportunities of young people and vulnerable members of communities in Vihiga County, Kenya.

The Foundation focuses on three interconnected areas:

  • Youth empowerment and economic opportunities
  • Education and skills development
  • Community health and health awareness

Babira Foundation recognizes that young people are an important part of the future of Vihiga County. However, many young people face challenges including limited access to employment opportunities, inadequate skills, financial constraints, and limited access to mentorship and career guidance.

At the same time, barriers to education and health information can affect the ability of individuals and families to improve their livelihoods and quality of life.

The Foundation therefore seeks to work with communities, schools, government institutions, development partners, private organizations, donors and other stakeholders to develop practical and sustainable programmes that respond to local needs.

As a developing organization, Babira Foundation aims to build its programmes through strong community participation, transparency, accountability and partnerships.

1.3 Our Vision

A healthy, educated and economically empowered community where young people have the opportunity to reach their full potential.

1.4 Our Mission

To empower young people and vulnerable communities in Vihiga County through education, skills development, economic opportunities, mentorship and improved access to health information and community-based support.

1.5 Our Core Purpose

Babira Foundation exists to help communities identify challenges, develop practical solutions and create opportunities that enable individuals particularly young people to become more self-reliant and contribute positively to society.

Page 2 - Our Objectives and Core Values

2.1 Main Objectives

Babira Foundation seeks to:

  • Empower young people with practical, entrepreneurial, digital and vocational skills.
  • Promote access to education and learning opportunities.
  • Support vulnerable learners and communities facing barriers to education.
  • Promote career guidance, mentorship and personal development among young people.
  • Encourage entrepreneurship and income-generating activities.
  • Promote financial literacy among youth and community members.
  • Create awareness about preventive healthcare and healthy lifestyles.
  • Support community health education and appropriate referrals.
  • Build partnerships that create opportunities for young people and vulnerable communities.
  • Encourage community participation in identifying and addressing local challenges.
  • Promote sustainable development and self-reliance.
  • Advocate for inclusive opportunities for young people, women and other vulnerable members of society.

2.2 Our Core Values

Integrity - We conduct our work honestly, responsibly and transparently.

Accountability - We take responsibility for the resources entrusted to us and the results of our programmes.

Empowerment - We believe communities should be equipped with knowledge, skills and opportunities that enable them to improve their own lives.

Inclusion - We seek to ensure that programmes are accessible to people regardless of their social or economic circumstances.

Respect - We respect the dignity, rights, opinions and experiences of the communities we serve.

Partnership - We believe sustainable community development requires collaboration between communities, government, donors, private organizations and other stakeholders.

Sustainability - We seek solutions that can continue creating benefits beyond the initial project funding period.

Page 4 - Community Health and Development

4.1 Community Health Programme

Babira Foundation recognizes that healthy communities are essential for education, productivity and sustainable development.

The Foundation will promote health awareness and community-level initiatives while working with qualified health professionals and relevant institutions where specialized medical services are required.

  • Community health awareness campaigns
  • Health education sessions
  • Preventive health awareness
  • Hygiene and sanitation education
  • Nutrition awareness
  • Youth health education
  • Community outreach activities
  • Health screening partnerships where appropriate
  • Referral and linkage to appropriate health facilities
  • Awareness on available health services

The Foundation will work with qualified professionals and relevant health institutions when implementing activities requiring medical expertise.

4.2 Integrated Community Development

Babira Foundation recognizes that youth empowerment, education and health are interconnected. For example, a young person who has access to education and skills may have better opportunities to earn a livelihood. Economic empowerment can in turn improve a household ability to support education and access appropriate healthcare.

The Foundation therefore seeks to implement integrated community programmes rather than treating development challenges in isolation.

4.3 Target Beneficiaries

  • Young people
  • Students and learners
  • Unemployed and underemployed youth
  • Young entrepreneurs
  • Vulnerable households
  • Women and girls
  • Children facing barriers to education
  • Community members requiring health information
  • Other vulnerable groups identified through community needs assessments

Specific beneficiary groups will be determined according to the objectives and requirements of each project.

Page 5 - Geographical Focus and Implementation Approach

5.1 Geographical Focus

Babira Foundation is focused on Vihiga County, Western Kenya.

The Foundation intends to work with communities across the county according to identified needs, available resources and programme priorities.

As the organization grows, programmes may be expanded through partnerships to other areas where appropriate.

5.2 Community-Based Approach

Babira Foundation believes that successful development programmes should involve the people they are intended to serve. The Foundation will therefore seek to:

  • Identify community needs.
  • Consult relevant community stakeholders.
  • Develop practical interventions.
  • Implement activities with community participation.
  • Monitor progress.
  • Collect feedback from beneficiaries.
  • Document lessons and results.
  • Improve future programmes based on evidence.

5.3 Partnerships

Babira Foundation seeks to establish partnerships with:

  • County and national government institutions
  • Schools and educational institutions
  • Health facilities and qualified health professionals
  • Community-based organizations
  • Faith-based organizations
  • Youth groups
  • Women groups
  • Private-sector organizations
  • Local businesses
  • Development organizations
  • National and international NGOs
  • Foundations and charitable organizations
  • Individual donors
  • Training institutions

Partnerships will enable the Foundation to combine resources, expertise, networks and opportunities for greater community impact.

5.4 Sustainability

Babira Foundation will seek to develop programmes that can continue generating benefits after external funding ends. Sustainability approaches may include:

  • Skills transfer
  • Community ownership
  • Youth entrepreneurship
  • Local partnerships
  • Volunteer engagement
  • Capacity building
  • Resource mobilization
  • Linking beneficiaries with existing opportunities
  • Development of community-led initiatives

Page 6 - Monitoring, Accountability and Funding

6.1 Monitoring and Evaluation

Babira Foundation will establish appropriate monitoring and evaluation systems for its programmes. Depending on the project, indicators may include:

  • Number of youth trained
  • Number of learners supported
  • Number of mentorship sessions conducted
  • Number of community members reached
  • Number of entrepreneurship activities supported
  • Number of health-awareness sessions conducted
  • Number of beneficiaries referred to appropriate services
  • Percentage of participants completing training
  • Participant feedback and satisfaction
  • Changes in knowledge and skills following programme activities

Project-specific indicators will be established before implementation.

6.2 Accountability and Transparency

  • Proper financial records
  • Appropriate authorization procedures
  • Project budgets
  • Supporting documentation for expenditure
  • Procurement procedures
  • Regular programme reports
  • Financial reporting
  • Donor reporting
  • Appropriate organizational policies
  • Monitoring and evaluation records

The Foundation will comply with applicable Kenyan laws and regulatory requirements.

6.3 Funding Priorities

Youth Empowerment - Funding for skills training, entrepreneurship, mentorship, digital literacy and employment-readiness programmes.

Education - Funding for educational support, learning materials, mentorship, career guidance and vulnerable learners.

Community Health - Funding for health awareness, community outreach, preventive health education and partnerships with qualified health service providers.

Institutional Capacity - Support for strengthening organizational systems, staff and volunteer capacity, monitoring and evaluation, technology and programme development.

6.4 Types of Support

  • Grants
  • Project funding
  • Corporate social responsibility partnerships
  • Donations
  • In-kind support
  • Equipment and learning materials
  • Technical assistance
  • Training partnerships
  • Volunteer support
  • Strategic partnerships

Page 7 - Partnership Opportunity and Contact

7.1 Why Partner With Babira Foundation

Babira Foundation seeks to become a trusted community partner in Vihiga County by connecting resources and expertise with locally identified needs.

Partnership with the Foundation can provide an opportunity to support programmes focused on:

Youth - Skills - Opportunities - Economic empowerment

Education - Knowledge - Confidence - Future opportunities

Health awareness - Prevention - Healthy communities - Productivity

The Foundation welcomes partnerships based on shared objectives, transparency, measurable outcomes and community benefit.

7.2 Our Commitment to Donors and Partners

  • Using resources responsibly.
  • Maintaining appropriate financial records.
  • Providing programme updates and reports.
  • Monitoring project activities and outcomes.
  • Maintaining open communication with partners.
  • Protecting the dignity of beneficiaries.
  • Promoting responsible safeguarding practices.
  • Learning from programme implementation.
  • Using evidence to improve future interventions.

7.3 Our Long-Term Goal

Our long-term goal is to contribute to a Vihiga County where young people have access to skills and opportunities, learners are supported to pursue education, and communities have better access to health information and appropriate services.

Babira Foundation aims to grow gradually through strong partnerships, community participation and accountable programme implementation.
