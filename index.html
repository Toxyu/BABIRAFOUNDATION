About Babira Foundation

Introduction

Babira Foundation is a community-focused non-profit organization committed to improving lives and opportunities of young people and vulnerable members in Vihiga County, Kenya. Focus on youth empowerment, education and skills development, community health.

Our Vision

A healthy, educated and economically empowered community where young people have opportunity to reach full potential.

Our Mission

To empower young people and vulnerable communities in Vihiga County through education, skills development, economic opportunities, mentorship and improved access to health information.

Youth Empowerment Programme

Equipping young people with practical skills for employment and entrepreneurship. Activities: Entrepreneurship, digital skills, financial literacy, career guidance, mentorship, leadership, life-skills, vocational linkages, employment-readiness, business idea development, networking.

Expected Outcomes

  • Increased employability
  • Entrepreneurship knowledge
  • Financial management
  • Mentorship access
  • Awareness of opportunities
  • Confidence and leadership

Education Programme

Supporting education by addressing barriers. Activities: Education support, learning materials, mentorship, career guidance, academic motivation, outreach, digital literacy, vulnerable learners support, awareness campaigns, school partnerships.

Community Health and Development

Healthy communities essential for education and productivity. Activities: Health awareness campaigns, education sessions, preventive health, hygiene, nutrition, youth health, outreach, screening partnerships, referrals, awareness on services. Integrated approach with target beneficiaries: youth, students, unemployed, entrepreneurs, vulnerable households, women and girls.

Geographical Focus and Implementation

Focused on Vihiga County, Western Kenya. Community-based approach: identify needs, consult stakeholders, develop interventions, implement with participation, monitor, collect feedback, document lessons, improve based on evidence. Partnerships with government, schools, health facilities, CBOs, youth groups, private sector, NGOs, donors. Sustainability via skills transfer, ownership, entrepreneurship, partnerships, volunteer engagement.
