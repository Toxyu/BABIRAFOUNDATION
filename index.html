Main Objectives & Values

• Empower young people with practical, entrepreneurial, digital, and vocational skills.

• Promote access to education and learning opportunities for vulnerable learners.

• Encourage youth entrepreneurship, financial literacy, and income-generating activities.

• Create awareness about preventive healthcare, hygiene, and healthy lifestyles.

Youth Empowerment Programme

Equipping young people with practical skills, digital training, financial literacy, and business management to improve employability and self-reliance.

Education Programme

Addressing barriers that prevent children and young people from accessing or benefiting fully from learning opportunities across Vihiga County.

Agriculture & Livelihood

Supporting sustainable farming, market linkages and Vihiga County food security.
